Noise Dream

A generator of sounds for sleep. The app synthesises noise in real time: ventilation, a compressor, lamps, rain, a stream, dripping and wind chimes.

In development Apache-2.0

The application has not been released yet. This page fills in when the first version ships.

Why

Ordinary noise recordings are short, made on a phone whose battery is nearly flat, and audibly hiss during quiet passages. You have to loop them, and after half an hour the seam becomes noticeable. Here the sound is assembled as it plays, so the loop is seamless and the volume can be changed smoothly.
